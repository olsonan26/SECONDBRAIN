# Secondbrain design

The workspace is a quiet observatory around a luminous brain-shaped knowledge graph. Its visual drama belongs to the corpus itself: cyan, violet, coral, and warm-gold communities on deep ink. Supporting panels are restrained and readable.

Palette: ink #080b16, panel #101626, text #eef3ff, cyan #5ce5ec, violet #a68aff, coral #ff8ba2. Typeface: locally packaged Manrope for the interface, system sans fallback. Large numbers are subordinate to real content and useful controls.

Desktop: a narrow navigation rail, wide graph or working canvas, and a source inspector. Mobile: horizontal navigation and stacked panels. The graph responds to dragging and zooming; reduced-motion preference disables idle rotation. Every meaningful graph relationship retains its extraction confidence.

The first screen is the brain, not a marketing landing page. Search, sources, writing, and workflows share one evidence library. Context-budget figures describe actual retrieved text lengths, never invented dollar savings.
